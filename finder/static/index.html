Find claims

Here you search for topics you are interested in, read the claims from HoVer that match each one, and select 112 of them as your claims for the whole homework. You need exactly 112, with at least 30 supported and 30 not supported, so that an agent that always gives the same answer cannot score well.

A search by keyword finds the claims that contain your words, and a search by meaning finds the claims whose words are close in meaning to yours, even when they share none of them. Both combines the two rankings.

The search by meaning uses word vectors, lists of numbers that are close together for words used in similar ways; this is what an embedding search is. Each claim is searched by its own text, the titles of the Wikipedia articles it needs, and those articles' one-line descriptions.

How to search

Search a topic you are interested in. Its claims will appear here, best first, and you select the ones that are on your topic.

    Your selection

    Your selection stays in this browser until you save it. Your saved claims are in data/dev.jsonl and data/eval.jsonl. Saving again replaces them, until the agent has run on them in step 1c.
