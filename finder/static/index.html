How to search
How the search works

The claim finder is a small retrieval system. Its code is in finder/search.py, and step 0b asks you to read it.

  • A search by keyword finds the claims that contain your words.
  • A search by meaning is an embedding search. It finds the claims whose words are close in meaning to yours, so a claim can match without sharing a word with your search.
  • Both combines the two rankings into one.
  • Each claim is searched by its text, the titles of the Wikipedia articles it needs, and those articles' one-line descriptions.
